VEX (Vulnerability Exploitability eXchange) information communicates
decisions about vulnerability applicability.

VDR information provides vulnerability-related data exchange support.

## Export Purpose

Exports help teams share structured vulnerability information with other
tools or stakeholders.

## Important Note

The availability and content of exports depend on configured Aura
capabilities.

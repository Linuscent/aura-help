Aura provides compliance-related workflows that help teams connect
product security information with regulatory requirements.

## Supported Regulatory Context

Regulatory information may include frameworks relevant to product
cybersecurity.

Teams can use this information to support internal security reviews and
documentation activities.

## Responsibility

Regulatory compliance decisions remain the responsibility of the
organization using Aura.

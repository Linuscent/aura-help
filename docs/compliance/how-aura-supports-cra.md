Aura supports organizations preparing cybersecurity information related
to the Cyber Resilience Act.

## Supported Activities

Aura can help teams:

-   Organize product security information
-   Track vulnerabilities
-   Maintain evidence
-   Review security decisions

## Evidence-Based Approach

CRA-related workflows require traceable information. Aura helps connect
security activities with supporting evidence.

## Limitation

Aura supports preparation and management of cybersecurity information
but does not provide legal certification.

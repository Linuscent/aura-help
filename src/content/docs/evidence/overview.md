Aura Evidence provides a workspace for organizing cybersecurity evidence
and supporting compliance workflows.

Evidence helps teams collect, review, and manage information related to
security requirements.

## Open Evidence in the upcoming navigation

The upcoming dashboard navigation moves release-level evidence into the selected
release's **Compliance** workspace. It may not yet be available in your deployment.

1. Open **Products** and choose a product release.
2. From the **release overview**, open **Compliance workspace**.
3. Select the **Evidence** tab and confirm that you are working with the intended
   product and release.

If your deployment does not yet show **Compliance workspace** and **Evidence**
is still in the left navigation, select **Evidence**, then choose an authorized
release.

**Evidence** will be a tab, not a standalone sidebar item. Release Compliance
workflows depend on your workspace plan and permissions, with Pro and Team access
by default.

**Reporting** stays in primary navigation for confidential CRA case evidence.
Release Evidence and Reporting cases are separate workflows; opening Compliance
does not disclose release evidence to a case.

## Evidence and Compliance

Evidence supports internal review processes and helps teams connect
security activities with applicable requirements.

Evidence should be treated as supporting information for internal
decisions and review workflows.

## Current Scope

The Evidence area may provide visibility into:

-   Evidence collection workflows
-   Requirement review
-   Internal documentation support

You can explicitly capture a saved technical vulnerability assessment as an
immutable Evidence record and select it to support an internal release decision.
The historical snapshot remains separate from the current finding. Saving an
assessment alone does not create Evidence. See the
[Evidence Workflow](/evidence/evidence-workflow/) for the steps and permissions.

Compliance readiness indicators and evidence workflows depend on the
available Aura capabilities and configured data.

## On the Free plan

Evidence workflows are part of Pro and Team. In the upcoming navigation, Free
users opening release Compliance will see the existing **Evidence on Pro**
explanation, with links to what they can already do:

- review and assess vulnerabilities on the Risks page;
- prepare reporting cases in **Reporting**.

It also lists what Pro adds, such as evidence collection, approvals, release
decisions, VEX exports and Trust Center. Workspace owners see an upgrade link;
other members are asked to contact the owner.

An evidence chain connects security activities with the information used
to support decisions.

## Purpose

Maintaining an evidence chain helps teams understand:

-   What information was reviewed
-   Which decision was made
-   When the decision occurred
-   Who was involved

## Security Context

Evidence traceability supports internal reviews, audits, and compliance
preparation.

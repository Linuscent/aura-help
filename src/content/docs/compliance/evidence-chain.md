An evidence chain connects security activities with the information used
to support decisions.

## Purpose

Maintaining an evidence chain helps teams understand:

-   What information was reviewed
-   Which decision was made
-   When the decision occurred
-   Who was involved

For vulnerability assessments, an explicit Evidence capture retains the saved
assessment and its capture attribution. An internal release decision can cite
that record and retain its historical contents even if the current finding changes.
Capture attribution is not the original assessment author or decision time.
See [Evidence Workflow](/evidence/evidence-workflow/) for selecting supporting
Evidence; saving a finding does not automatically create this chain.

## Security Context

Evidence traceability supports internal reviews, audits, and compliance
preparation.

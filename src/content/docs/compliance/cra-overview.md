The European Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) introduces cybersecurity
requirements for products with digital elements.

Manufacturers need to manage cybersecurity throughout the product
lifecycle, including:

-   Secure development practices
-   Vulnerability handling
-   Security documentation
-   Post-market activities

## Aura and CRA

Aura helps teams organize security information and evidence that can
support CRA-related workflows.

## Important Note

Using Aura does not automatically make a product CRA compliant.
Organizations remain responsible for meeting applicable legal and
regulatory requirements.

## Choose the right workflow

Aura supports organizations preparing cybersecurity information related to the Cyber Resilience Act. Confidential **Reporting** and paid **Compliance** serve different purposes; neither certifies legal compliance.

## Supported Activities

Aura can help teams:

-   Organize product security information
-   Track vulnerabilities
-   Maintain evidence
-   Review security decisions

## Evidence-Based Approach

CRA-related workflows require traceable information. Aura helps connect
security activities with supporting evidence.

## Prepare confidential reports on any plan

**Reporting** is available on Free, Pro and Team to help manufacturers prepare and track confidential reports. A case can describe products manually without an SBOM; those descriptions do not create products or releases in your inventory.

The guided workflow supports:

1. Human assessments of actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents, with recorded reasons and awareness times. Opening a case does not make it reportable.
2. Manually confirmed deadlines, including their source and rationale. Aura does not calculate legal deadlines.
3. Saved report drafts and explicit approval of the current revision. Changes to relevant facts can invalidate approval; moving between steps does not save or approve work automatically.
4. Private case evidence and exports of explicitly selected evidence. Files are unscanned, and exporting a package is not submission.
5. Recording an external filing after a representative submits through the appropriate authority channel, including the submission reference and optional receipt. Aura does not submit to authorities, and its export is not a validated import format for ENISA's Single Reporting Platform.

Case access is separate from ordinary workspace membership. **Viewers** can read their authorized cases; **contributors** can also prepare drafts and add evidence; **managers** can assess, confirm deadlines, approve, export, record submissions and manage case access. Workspace owners and admins have audited case oversight and manage private manufacturer profiles. Other members see only cases they are authorized to access.

Choose an exercise for practice rather than a live case. Exercise reports and filing records are explicitly simulated; exercises never send reminder emails. The exercise setting is fixed when the case is created.

## Keep Reporting separate from paid Compliance

On Pro and Team, **Compliance** supports evidence and control assessments, approvals and internal release decisions, subject to the relevant permissions. Rich VEX/VDR and Trust Center also remain separate paid capabilities.

Free Reporting and all-plan technical vulnerability triage do not unlock these paid resources. Linking source information to a report does not bypass its access rules. Reporting also retains its own file, storage and export limits, even when your plan has unlimited product, release or user counts.

## Limitation

Aura guides preparation and record-keeping, not automatic legal judgment. Your organization remains responsible for applicability, reportability, deadline confirmation, approval and external filing. Aura does not automatically submit authority reports or broadcast customer advisories, and an export, approval or closed case is not legal certification.

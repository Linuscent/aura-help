Regulations is a read-only educational reference for understanding regulatory
context. It is not a release Compliance workspace or a place to collect evidence,
record decisions, or prepare confidential Reporting cases.

## Open the reference in the upcoming navigation

The upcoming dashboard navigation may not yet be available in your deployment.
You will be able to open Regulations from:

- **Help → Reference → Regulations**, in the Help footer;
- **Regulations reference**, in a release's **Compliance** workspace.

A regular click on the Help footer's **Regulations** reference closes Help and
navigates to the Regulations page, even if the Help article is unavailable. This
is different from an in-panel article link, which opens another article inside
Help. Modifier clicks leave Help open.

Normal Help behavior remains unchanged: **Back** returns to the previous article
inside the panel, and closing Help or pressing **Escape** returns to your unchanged
dashboard work when you have not followed the reference out of the panel.

## Supported Regulatory Context

Regulatory information may include frameworks relevant to product
cybersecurity.

Teams can use this information to support internal security reviews and
documentation activities.

## Responsibility

Regulatory compliance decisions remain the responsibility of the
organization using Aura.

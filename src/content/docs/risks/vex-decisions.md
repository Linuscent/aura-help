VEX information communicates decisions about vulnerability applicability
and handling.

## Purpose

VEX decisions help teams document whether a vulnerability affects a
product and what action should be taken.

## Decision Context

A VEX decision may include:

-   Applicability assessment
-   Justification
-   Response information

VEX workflows depend on available product and vulnerability data.

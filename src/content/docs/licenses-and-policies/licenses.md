Aura provides visibility into software license information associated
with components in a product release.

License information helps teams understand open-source software usage
and identify potential licensing concerns.

## License Information

License data may include:

-   Component license information
-   License identifiers

## Reviewing Licenses

Teams can review license information to:

-   Understand software obligations
-   Identify missing or unusual license information
-   Support governance workflows

## Important Note

License results depend on the information available from the uploaded
SBOM and connected analysis capabilities.

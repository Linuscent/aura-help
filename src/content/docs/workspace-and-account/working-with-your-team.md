Aura supports collaboration by allowing teams to work together inside a
shared workspace.

## Team Collaboration

Teams can collaborate on:

-   Product security information
-   Findings review
-   Evidence workflows
-   Security decisions

## Access Management

Team collaboration depends on workspace membership and user permissions.

## Inviting teammates

Workspace owners and admins invite people from **Workspace Settings →
Invitations**. The form shows how many seats are used, counting members and
pending invitations.

When every seat is used, inviting is disabled:

- owners see an **Upgrade plan** link to Billing;
- admins are asked to contact the workspace owner.

Each plan includes a set number of users. See
[Access and Permissions](/workspace-and-account/access-and-permissions/).

## Respond to an invitation

Pending workspace invitations sent to your account's email address appear in
two places:

- a banner on **Home**;
- the **Pending invitations** list in **Account → Profile**.

Select **Accept** to join the workspace. Aura then switches you to it. If the
invitation is accepted but the workspace cannot be selected, Aura says so and
shows **Select workspace**; you do not need to accept again.

Select **Reject** to decline the invitation. Rejecting is permanent for that
invitation: it disappears from both places, and a workspace owner or admin must
send a new invitation if you change your mind.

### Close the Home banner

Closing the Home banner only hides the current invitations in this browser for
your account. It does not reject them. They stay in **Account → Profile**, where
you can still accept or reject them. A new invitation shows the banner again.

The banner's closed state is stored locally, so another browser or device can
still show it. If browser storage is unavailable, the banner stays closed only
for your current visit.

Expired invitations and invitations to workspaces that are no longer active are
not listed. If an invitation you expect is missing, check that you are signed in
with the invited email address, then ask the workspace owner or admin to send a
new invitation.

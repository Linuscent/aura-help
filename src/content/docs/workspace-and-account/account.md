The account area contains user-specific information and account-related
settings.

## Account Management

Users can manage available account information and personal preferences
according to the capabilities enabled in Aura.

## Pending invitations

**Account → Profile** lists every pending workspace invitation sent to your
account, including invitations you hid by closing the Home banner. Accept or
reject them here. See
[Respond to an invitation](/workspace-and-account/working-with-your-team/#respond-to-an-invitation).

## Access

Your account settings are available even if you do not belong to any workspace
yet, so you can respond to invitations before creating or joining one.
Workspace information and actions still depend on workspace membership and
assigned permissions.

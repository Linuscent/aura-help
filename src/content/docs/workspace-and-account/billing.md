## Find subscriptions you pay for

Open **Account → Billing**, then select a workspace under **Subscriptions you
pay for**. This list follows the subscriptions you fund, not your active
workspace or workspace role. You can still manage billing and cancel a
subscription after losing workspace membership; this does not restore access
to workspace data.

Review the plan, billing interval, status, billing period, and renewal or end
date before taking action. If live details are unavailable, follow the displayed
retry or support guidance rather than assuming a payment action succeeded.

## Update payments or renewal

Use the controls available for the selected subscription and review their
confirmation before proceeding:

- **Update payment method** changes the payment method for the subscription.
  For a past-due subscription, this may also collect the outstanding balance.
- **Cancel subscription** keeps the paid plan until the displayed end date,
  then the workspace moves to Free. Workspace owners are notified.
- **Continue renewal** reverses a scheduled cancellation and resumes future
  subscription payments. It is offered only when the subscription is eligible.

If a payment change cannot be confirmed, refresh and review the current
subscription state. Do not submit the change again; contact support if it does
not update shortly, following the displayed guidance. Do not assume a timeout
means no change or charge occurred.

## Review transactions and invoices

Under **Transaction history**, select a transaction to inspect its details.
Choose **Load more** for older transactions and **Download invoice (PDF)** when
available. Not every transaction has an invoice.

Details can include line items, discounts, tax, credits, amounts paid or
outstanding, refunds and adjustments, and billing-contact information. A
transaction's historical payment method may differ from the method used for
future renewals. These views do not let you initiate refunds or edit historical
billing-contact details.

## Understand workspace billing permissions

Workspace owners use **Workspace Settings → Billing** to review workspace
access and available plans. Starting a subscription requires confirming
**I'll pay for this subscription from my personal billing account**.

For subscriptions linked to a personal billing account, workspace ownership
does not expose another person's payment details or grant their cancellation
rights. Owners can see who funds the subscription. Changing the plan requires
being both its payer and a current workspace owner; use **Change plan in
Workspace Settings** when available.

Older subscriptions not yet linked to a personal billing account retain their
workspace-owner management path until migrated. If a subscription does not
appear under your personal billing account, check Workspace Settings and follow
the displayed guidance rather than assuming it has ended.

Review prorated charges, credits, and tax before **Confirm upgrade**. Plan or
interval changes during a trial are unavailable. Continuing a scheduled
cancellation resumes renewal independently of any later upgrade.

Transferring the subscription's payer is not a self-service action in these
screens. If a subscription is missing or its billing identity has changed,
follow the displayed support guidance.
